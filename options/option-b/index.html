About us

At Urban Arch Studio, we believe every space has a story — and we design yours with creativity, clarity and care. We put people first: every project begins with your aspirations and context, and becomes a place that is functional, timeless and creative.

Based in Mumbai and Ranchi, we are a multidisciplinary design practice specializing in Architecture, Interiors, Landscape, Urban Design and Master Planning. Whether it’s a luxury home, a modern workplace, or a community-driven development, our designs combine aesthetic elegance, practical functionality, and sustainable innovation.

We don’t just create buildings — we craft environments where life unfolds, experiences deepen, and memories are made.

Our approach

Every projectis a dialogue.

At UAS, we treat every project as a dialogue—between context and creativity, client and designer, tradition and innovation. Our work is driven by a deep respect for scale, proportion, material, and light, and our process ensures every space we design reflects the aspirations and lifestyle of its users.

Whether it’s a modern apartment in Mumbai or a contextual home in Ranchi, our team brings a consistent focus on clarity, craftsmanship, and client collaboration.

The people

The founders

Utkarsh

Architect & Co-founder

Utkarsh is an architect and co-founder at Urban Arch Studio. A graduate of L.S. Raheja School of Architecture, he has worked with leading firms in Mumbai, gaining experience across architecture, interiors, and urban design. Passionate about sustainability, he strives to create eco-friendly solutions that reflect both client aspirations and environmental responsibility. His interests extend to photography, travel, and gardening, which inspire his creative approach to design.

Sonali

Architect, Urban Researcher & Co-founder

Sonali is an architect, urban researcher, and co-founder at Urban Arch Studio. With a Master’s in Urban Design from Oxford Brookes University, UK, and a degree in Architecture from L.S. Raheja School of Architecture, she brings expertise in sustainable design, master planning, high-rise residential projects, and interiors. Passionate about cities, communities, and the environment, she blends research with practice to create responsive and meaningful spaces.
